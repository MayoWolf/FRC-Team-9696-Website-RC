`public/assets/hami-mascot.png` is the existing robot mascot displayed on the [Hamilton Hamichanics public HCB fundraiser](https://hcb.hackclub.com/donations/start/9696-hami-hamichanics), retrieved October 30, 2026. Used as the team's identity for this team website. The website's MIT license does not apply to this asset. Rights remain with the original rights holder; contact the team regarding other uses.
