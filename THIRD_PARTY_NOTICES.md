# Third-party assets

## HAMI mascot

`public/assets/hami-mascot.png` is the existing robot mascot displayed on the [Hamilton Hamichanics public HCB fundraiser](https://hcb.hackclub.com/donations/start/9696-hami-hamichanics), retrieved October 30, 2026. Used as the team's identity for this team website. The robot icon in `public/assets/icon.svg` is a simplified vector adaptation of that team mascot. The website's MIT license does not apply to these team identity assets. Rights remain with the original rights holder; contact the team regarding other uses.
